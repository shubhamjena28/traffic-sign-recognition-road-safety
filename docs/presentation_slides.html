Problem Statement & Automotive Perception Challenges
Why Traditional Vision Pipelines Fail in Real-World Automotive Hardware
⚠ Human Driver Fatigue
Over 90% of highway accidents involve human error, inattention, or overlooked regulatory signs (halt signs, blind curves, speed zones).
⚙ Embedded ECU Limits
Vehicles lack cloud GPU clusters. Perception algorithms must execute on low-power CPUs within strict latency limits (≤ 150 ms) without OpenCV native DLL dependencies.
❄ Adverse Weather
Rain streaks, solar glare, and nighttime contrast drop severely degrade edge gradients, risking catastrophic false negatives on critical halt signs.

Mathematical Formulations & Artificial Neurons
Pure Java Implementation of Core Perception Math & Neural Units
◆ Spatial Geometry & Greedy NMS
  • Intersection over Union (Jaccard Index):
    IoU(A, B) = Area(A ∩ B) / Area(A ∪ B)
  • Greedy NMS: Suppresses candidate proposals sharing IoU ≥ 0.50 with higher-confidence anchors.
  • Aspect-Preserving Letterboxing: Resizes non-square inputs to 640x640 with uniform gray padding (114, 114, 114).
◆ Artificial Neuron Demonstrations
  • McCulloch-Pitts Discrete Threshold Gates:
    y = Θ(Σ w_i x_i - θ)
    Verified logic gates: AND, OR, NOT, NAND, XOR.
  • Continuous Sigmoid Neuron & Derivative:
    σ(z) = 1 / (1 + e^-z)
    σ'(z) = σ(z) · (1 - σ(z))

Temporal Sequence Tracking & LSTM Risk Valuation
Kinematic Distance Proxy & Multi-Frame Sequential Risk Prediction
◆ Box Growth Delta as Distance Proxy
  • As the vehicle approaches a sign, projected box area scales quadratically: Area_t ∝ 1 / d_t^2.
  • The differential growth rate ΔArea_t = Area_t - Area_{t-1} serves as a reliable vehicle closing proxy.
  • Positive, accelerating growth rates flag imminent hazard intersection.
◆ 2-Layer LSTM Sequence Risk Model
  • Evaluates 10-frame sliding windows (sign count, class, max area, Δarea).
  • Predicts dynamic road risk levels: LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH.
  • Activation Function Comparison:
    - Tanh: 89.35% Accuracy (0.9030 F1) [WINNER]
    - Step: 89.05% Accuracy (0.9012 F1)
    - Sigmoid: 62.72% Accuracy (Vanishing gradient)

Rare-Class Augmentation via Conditional VAE
Overcoming Long-Tail Imbalance Through Class-Conditioned Latent Synthesis
◆ Long-Tail Imbalance Problem
  • Natural driving sets have severe imbalance: speed signs have 2,000+ samples, while rare hazard signs (Pedestrian, Uneven Road) have fewer than 180.
  • Models biased against rare signs risk dangerous false negatives on critical warnings.
◆ Empirical CVAE Performance Gain
  • Trained a Conditional VAE on rare GTSRB classes.
  • Rare-Class Recall (Baseline): 75.9%
  • Rare-Class Recall (CVAE Augmented): 91.0%
  • Absolute Safety Improvement: +15.1% Recall Gain
  • t-SNE latent manifold confirms high cluster separability.

Responsible-Use Limits & Operational Domain (ODD)
Ethical Operational Boundaries & Domain Adaptation Roadmap
🚗 Driver Aid Only
  • Level 2 ADAS advisory warnings only (visual HUD & audio chimes).
  • Never connect directly to steering, throttle, or brake actuation.
  • Human driver retains legal custody and operational responsibility at all times.
🌧 Adverse Weather Trigger
  • Heavy rain streaks cause a 12.50% accuracy drop.
  • When windshield wipers operate on high or lux drops below threshold, system displays: "Perception Degraded — Driver Vigilance Required".
🌏 Geographic Domain Shift
  • Trained on European / Vienna Convention signs.
  • Prohibited from deployment on Indian roads without fine-tuning on Indian road datasets conforming to IRC-67 standards (multilingual scripts, unique hazard pictograms).
